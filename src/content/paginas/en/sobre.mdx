## Nature and status

The Centro de Estudos Econômicos (CEE) is a **student-run initiative for empirical economic analysis, with faculty support**, open to students from all campuses and the distance-learning track of the Economics programme at PUC Minas. It is neither a study group nor a consultancy: its outputs are reviewed publications and maintained data.

In its initial phases CEE operates as a **student initiative**: participation is voluntary and unpaid, with no formal affiliation, no registration requirement and no access to funding. Once it has a track record, it may be submitted as a university extension project — a decision scheduled for the month-9 review, not an automatic step.

## Mission

To produce rigorous, reproducible and open-access empirical economic analysis of the Brazilian economy, and to train undergraduate students to that standard, through a data observatory and publications subject to multi-layer review.

## Vision

To be recognised, within and beyond the University, as a student initiative whose data and analyses are cited by others, maintained by successive cohorts, and whose alumni carry this standard into graduate study and policymaking.

## Values

| Value | Operating rule |
|---|---|
| **Rigour** | No number is published unless a second person has reproduced it from the code. |
| **Transparency** | Open, citable and licensed data, code and methods; every quantitative claim states source, series and extraction date. |
| **Intellectual independence** | No partisan positions; CEE does not speak for the University; every publication carries a disclaimer; conflicts are declared. |
| **Progressive merit** | Open entry; responsibility (signing, reviewing, coordinating) grows with demonstrated quality, under criteria written in advance. |
| **Intellectual honesty** | Limitations stated in every product; errors corrected publicly through dated errata, with the original preserved. |
| **Training** | Student learning is an outcome of the Centre, not a by-product. |

## Analytical stance

CEE is an **analytical** centre, not an advocacy one. Policy recommendations appear only in **Technical Notes**, in a separate section, and every such note includes a section on what the evidence does **not** allow us to conclude. When CEE assesses proposals, it examines the quality of their formulation — for example, internal and fiscal consistency — never their political desirability. CEE does not produce its own GDP or inflation forecasts in its first year.

## Governance

Planned bodies: a faculty adviser; an Academic Council; an Editorial Committee of three senior members (none of them authors of the product under review); an Executive Coordination of two students with one-semester terms; Area Coordinators; a Training Directorate; and an Observatory maintainer with a reviewer. The bodies are not yet constituted; names will only be published with consent.

## Transparency

**Funding:** none. As a student initiative, CEE neither raises nor manages funds. **Conflicts of interest:** declared on joining and every semester. **Use of AI:** permitted and declared in every product; it never replaces human verification or independent re-execution of code, and no personal data is entered into external tools.

## Disclaimer

CEE is a student initiative. Its publications do not represent the position of PUC Minas or any other institution.
