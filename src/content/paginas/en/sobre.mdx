## Nature

The Centro de Estudos Econômicos (CEE) is a student academic society (*liga acadêmica*) of the Economics programme. It is non-profit, non-partisan and of indefinite duration, and is devoted to empirical economic research, to the academic training of its members and to sharing economic knowledge.

## Mission

<Destaque rotulo="Mission" icone="target">

To push forward the frontiers of knowledge and research on economic growth and development policy, to work with policymakers on the design of effective action, and to share its knowledge through teaching, analytical tools and publications, with the aim of contributing to inclusive prosperity in Brazil.

</Destaque>

<Lema fonte="Institutional motto · Statute, art. 4, §1">

Research and methods to drive economic growth and prosperity across Brazil.

</Lema>

## Vision

<Destaque rotulo="Vision" icone="eye">

A merit-based, collaborative and interconnected community of Economics students, able to push forward research on economic growth and development policy, to work with policymakers on the design of effective interventions, and to share knowledge through research, teaching, analytical tools and publications.

</Destaque>

**Horizon.** To be a reference in applied economic research done by students: rigorous, open and useful to Brazil's prosperity.

## How CEE contributes to public policy

CEE's work on public policy follows four steps (Statute, art. 50):

<Etapas orientacao="h" rotulo="Steps of the work on public policy">
  <Etapa titulo="Diagnose"><p>With reproducible data and methods.</p></Etapa>
  <Etapa titulo="Propose"><p>With recommendations kept separate from the evidence and accompanied by their limits.</p></Etapa>
  <Etapa titulo="Engage"><p>With those responsible for decisions.</p></Etapa>
  <Etapa titulo="Monitor"><p>Publishing what was and was not implemented.</p></Etapa>
</Etapas>

Public monitoring is limited to public targets that are measurable and verifiable with open data. Recommendations appear only in the outputs that allow them, in a separate section; all other outputs describe and analyse without recommending (see [Publications](../publicacoes/)).

<Cards cols={2}>

## Governance

CEE's bodies are the General Assembly, the Collegiate Board, the President and Vice-President, the Area Heads, the Programme Coordinators and Panel Leads, the Editorial Committee, the Advisory Council and the standing committees (Annual Evaluation, Electoral and Progression).

<Organograma lang="en" />

<Cards cols={3}>
  <Card titulo="General Assembly" icone="users"><p>Made up of active full members, it is the highest decision-making body. It meets in February to approve the annual plan and in November for accountability and elections.</p></Card>
  <Card titulo="Collegiate Board" icone="landmark"><p>President, Vice-President and up to seven Directors, elected by the General Assembly after a public hearing. It issues the Economic Assessment, a collegiate statement on the Brazilian economy.</p></Card>
  <Card titulo="Area Heads" icone="layers"><p>Research (under the Chief Economist), Data and Technology, Academic Training, Communication and Marketing, Events, Institutional and International Relations, and People and Administration, appointed by call and selection panel.</p></Card>
  <Card titulo="Programmes and panels" icone="compass"><p>Each active programme has a Coordinator and each active panel a Lead, appointed by call and selection panel (see <a href="../pesquisa/">Research</a>).</p></Card>
  <Card titulo="Editorial Committee" icone="shield-check" destaque><p>An independent body that approves every publication. The Collegiate Board cannot overturn an editorial decision or waive review; no author reviews or decides on their own work.</p></Card>
  <Card titulo="Advisory Council" icone="book-open" destaque><p>Made up of the faculty tutors (at least one, at most three), it follows the activities, issues opinions, judges appeals and nominates the members of the Editorial Committee.</p></Card>
</Cards>

Every selection for office follows a call published before applications open, with verifiable criteria; popularity, seniority and founder status are not criteria. Names of members are published only with their consent.

## Transparency and integrity

<Cards cols={2}>
  <Card titulo="Resources" icone="scale"><p>Setting up CEE does not presuppose raising or handling funds of its own. Any support, donation, grant or sponsorship requires approval by the General Assembly and the University's institutional procedures, with a record of purpose, origin, use and those responsible.</p></Card>
  <Card titulo="Conflicts of interest" icone="fingerprint"><p>Declared on joining, every semester and whenever a new situation arises. A link to an organisation directly assessed in an output bars the person from preparing, reviewing or deciding on it.</p></Card>
  <Card titulo="Artificial intelligence" icone="bot"><p>The use of AI tools is disclosed in each output, with the tool and purpose, while authorship, verification and responsibility remain human. Personal or restricted data are not entered into external tools without a legal basis and institutional authorisation.</p></Card>
  <Card titulo="Errata and Annual Report" icone="rotate-ccw"><p>A material error triggers a notice linked to the output within seven calendar days and an erratum with date, version and effects (see <a href="../erratas/">Errata</a>). CEE publishes an Annual Report every year, with its accounts and results.</p></Card>
</Cards>
