## Commitment

The CEE website aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA.

## Measures

Minimum contrast of 4.5:1 for text and 3:1 for graphical elements in light and dark modes; full keyboard navigation with visible focus and a skip link; a text description, alternative data table and CSV download for every chart; colour-blind-safe chart palettes with direct labels; accessible horizontally scrollable tables; respect for reduced-motion and dark-mode system preferences; automated axe and Lighthouse checks on every change.

## Reporting a problem

Please write to the [contact address](../contato/) or open an issue in the site's repository, stating the page and the problem.
