## Call for new members

<Box variant="note" title="First call">
  <p>The first call for new members has not yet been published. It will be announced on this site, with its terms. This site does not collect applications.</p>
</Box>

- The call opens in **February and August**, with an application period of at least ten calendar days.
- The form asks only for name, student number, programme, semester, programme of interest, weekly availability and a short motivation. No test, transcript or prior knowledge is required.
- The number of places follows supervision capacity: at most four Level 1 members per serving Panel Lead or Programme Coordinator.
- If there are more applicants than places, those who completed a CEE Study Group have priority; any remaining excess is settled by a **public draw**, recorded in minutes. Those not admitted go on a waiting list with priority in the next call.
- Academic performance, programme of origin and personal relationships with members are **not criteria** for entry.

Completing the track is required for Level 2.

**Study Groups.** Six weekly meetings on a topic, open to any student at the University, with at most twenty participants and admission by a motivation letter assessed against published criteria. Completion (attending five of the six meetings) earns a certificate and priority in the call for new members.

## Research career

Progression has five sequential, cumulative levels. A level is recognised by the Progression Committee, at the member's request, based on evidence, and measures proven output: a level does not confer office, and office does not confer a level.

| Level | Cumulative criteria | What it enables |
|---|---|---|
| **N1 — Participant** | Admission through the call | Supervised data tasks; co-authorship of Data Insights for an actual contribution |
| **N2 — Research Assistant** | Training track completed; two data tasks completed and documented; one numerical verification carried out | Building and documenting series; numerical verification of others' outputs; co-authorship of Business-Cycle Notes |
| **N3 — Analyst** | Two notes published, at least one approved in the first review round | Lead authorship of Business-Cycle Notes; maintaining panels; co-authorship of Technical Notes; coordinating a Study Group |
| **N4 — Associate Researcher** | One Technical Note published as lead author; three reviews with a written report | Lead authorship of Technical Notes and Discussion Papers; reviewing work in progress |
| **N5 — Senior Researcher** | One Discussion Paper published or submitted to a peer-reviewed journal or conference; two semesters as N4; proven supervision of two members | Supervising teams; proposing the agenda; leading flagship research projects |

The Committee decides within fifteen calendar days, with written reasons; appeals go to the Advisory Council within ten calendar days. Code, data curation and technical support receive their own credit but do not replace the outputs required for each level.

## Merit and office

Merit applies to progression and to office, never to entry. Every selection for office follows a call published before applications open, with criteria, weights and timetable that do not change during the process; only verifiable evidence counts (recognised level, published outputs, recorded deliverables); every panel includes at least one faculty tutor; each candidate receives the score and reasons for each criterion. Popularity, seniority and founder status are not criteria, and requirements are never lowered to fill a post.

Opportunities for tasks, authorship, review and training are announced with allocation criteria; holding them back to favour a candidate, ally or successor is forbidden.

## Call for Projects

At the start of each semester, for two to three weeks, the Call for Projects publishes one sheet per place, with project and panel, requirements, deliverables, duration, a weekly load of two to six hours, supervisor and what the participation yields in hours, authorship and progression. Each candidate may apply for up to three places. There is also an annual **flagship research** call, opening in August, for one or two projects of six to twelve months with faculty supervision.

## Cooperation with other institutions

- **Accredited collaborators** take part in a specific project, with a contribution plan, verified affiliation and an internal contact, for up to two renewable semesters. They do not vote, do not stand for office and only access the systems and data the project needs.
- **15 % cap.** Alumni and students from other institutions together make up at most fifteen per cent of the membership, checked before each accreditation with the inequality *x* ≤ 0.15 × (*N* + *x*), where *x* is the number of alumni and students from other institutions and *N* the number of other members. Once the cap is reached, new accreditations are suspended without removing anyone already taking part.
- **Joint projects** with research centres and groups at other institutions do not mean joining CEE: each student remains with their own organisation.
- CEE provides the documents needed to certify activities and hours (signed activity plan, validated hours log, completed deliverables, supervisor's assessment and a certificate through the authorised institutional channel); the home institution decides whether to recognise them.

## Time commitment and hours

- The indicative commitment is **two to six hours a week**, depending on the tasks taken on, following the academic calendar.
- Participation is academic, voluntary and free, with no pay for office. Grants, internships and other arrangements require their own instrument and the University's rules.
- Participation is logged monthly, with activity, role, hours and evidence. Certificates are issued through the authorised institutional channel; CEE **does not promise** credit towards the degree, extension hours or complementary activities before the programme coordination confirms it.
- After a full semester with no recorded participation, the member is notified and has ten days to respond before being marked inactive. Inactivity is not a sanction; returning depends on a request and on supervision capacity.

## Rights and duties

Full members may take part in activities suited to their level, receive guidance and formal recognition, propose topics and projects, access minutes and reports (except personal or restricted information), vote in the General Assembly when active and stand for office. They must follow the research, review, authorship and integrity protocols, document their tasks, protect the Centre's data and credentials and declare conflicts of interest.

## Personal data

Before opening any registration, CEE will publish a privacy notice stating purpose, legal basis, retention period, those responsible and how to exercise data rights. See the [privacy policy](../privacidade/).
