## Who can join

Undergraduate Economics students at PUC Minas, from any campus or the distance-learning track. There is no test, selection, or grade requirement, and no prior knowledge of econometrics or programming is expected — only willingness to learn and to have one's work reviewed by peers.

## How to join

The first open call has not yet been published, and this site has no sign-up form. Those interested can write to the [contact address](../contato/), stating their campus and area of interest (A, B or C). Each newcomer receives a small, real first task — documenting an Observatory series, checking a published figure, or building a chart with source and extraction date — reviewed with comments, not graded.

## Training track

Eight sessions per semester, open to all: Brazilian data sources and their pitfalls; reproducible workflows in R or Python; version control; PNAD microdata in practice; what each research design allows one to claim; applied growth accounting; technical writing; honest visualisation.

## Progression

Five levels — Participant, Research Assistant, Analyst, Associate Researcher and Area Coordinator — with objective criteria written in advance. Merit determines who signs, reviews and coordinates, not who may enter. Nobody is excluded for inactivity.

## What participation is not

It is **not an internship** and creates no employment or other formal relationship, nor any right to payment. There is no hierarchy, mandatory schedule or attendance control, and anyone may leave at any time.
