## Summary

This website **collects no personal data**, **uses no cookies**, **stores nothing in your browser** and **uses no audience analytics**. It loads no fonts, scripts or images from third-party servers, and has no forms.

## Hosting

The site is hosted as static files by Cloudflare, Inc. Like any web server, Cloudflare processes the IP address and technical request data to deliver the pages and protect the service, under the [Cloudflare privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). CEE does not enable Cloudflare's audience analytics or any equivalent and has no access to individual visitor records.

## Email

If you write to CEE, whatever you include in your message is used only to reply. It is not shared with third parties or entered into external AI tools, and is deleted on request or once no longer needed.

## Registrations and calls

Before opening any registration, such as the call for new members, CEE will publish a privacy notice stating purpose, legal basis, retention period, those responsible and how to exercise data rights. Personal data are kept only as long as needed and then deleted or anonymised, and access is restricted to those who need it (Internal Rules, art. 60).

## People on the site

Members' names appear only with recorded consent and can be removed on request at any time. Only name, role and body are published.

## Audience measurement

If CEE ever decides to measure audience, it will only use a tool without cookies or personal data, and this page will be updated before it is switched on.

## Microdata

CEE does not republish microdata that could identify people. Research involving primary data collection or identifiable data requires prior submission to a Research Ethics Committee.
