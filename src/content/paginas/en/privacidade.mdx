## Summary

This website **collects no personal data**, **uses no cookies** and **uses no audience analytics**. It loads no fonts, scripts or images from third-party servers.

## Stored in your browser

Only one optional preference: if you choose a light or dark theme in the footer, the choice is kept in your browser's `localStorage` under the key `cee-theme`. It never leaves your device.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security and operation, under the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). CEE has no access to those logs.

## Email contact

If you write to CEE, the data you include are used only to reply and organise participation, on the basis of your consent; they are not shared with third parties and are deleted on request. The data controller and retention period are still to be defined together with the University.
