## Production cycle

Every quantitative product follows six stages, always in this order: (1) choice of a question answerable with available public data; (2) a one-page **analytical design sheet**, filed and dated before any result is examined; (3) construction of data and code, with immutable raw data and numbered, versioned scripts; (4) **independent numerical verification** by a non-author, re-running the repository from scratch on a different machine; (5) a written content review by the faculty adviser; (6) publication with CRediT authorship, an open licence and a replication package — and a public, dated erratum if an error is found later.

## Three review layers

| Layer | Who | What | Approval criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Numerical verification | A member who is not an author | Re-runs the repository from scratch | Every table and figure is reproduced |
| 2. Content review | Faculty adviser | Method, interpretation, limitations | Written approval |
| 3. Editorial review | Editorial Committee | Clarity, citation, units, labels, licence | Checklist fully ticked |

Data Insights require layer 1; Business-Cycle Notes layers 1 and 3; Technical Notes all three; Working Papers all three plus an external review. The level of review depends on the ambition of the product, **never on the deadline**.

## Repository, vintages and citation

Every product uses the same repository structure (raw data, numbered scripts, processed data, outputs, README, data dictionary and environment lock file). Official series are revised by their sources, so every extraction is archived with its date (a *vintage*), allowing published results to be reproduced with the data as they stood. Observatory editions are numbered, carry a change log and, when available, a DOI.

## Errata

Errors found after publication produce a **public, dated erratum** attached to the original, which remains accessible. Silent correction is a serious breach. The target is not zero errata — that would reward hiding mistakes — but an interval of **less than seven days** between identification and publication of the erratum. See the [errata register](../erratas/).

## Authorship, conflicts and AI

Contributions are declared by role using the CRediT taxonomy. Members declare political affiliations, jobs and relevant internships on joining and every semester, and step aside from products involving organisations they are linked to. AI tools may be used and must be declared; all analytical judgements are verified by people, AI-generated code goes through the same independent re-execution, no personal data is entered into external tools, and AI tools are never listed as authors.
