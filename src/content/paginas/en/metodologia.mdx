## Editorial workflow

Every output follows, in this order, the workflow set in the Internal Rules (art. 46):

<Etapas rotulo="Steps of the editorial workflow">
  <Etapa titulo="Proposal registration"><p>With the Programme Coordinator, stating question, sources, period and method (Manual, Annex G), and the programme and Manual question it answers.</p></Etapa>
  <Etapa titulo="Preparation"><p>With data and code in a CEE repository, with version history.</p></Etapa>
  <Etapa titulo="Numerical verification"><p>By someone other than the authors, who re-runs the code and checks every number, table and chart, with a verification report.</p></Etapa>
  <Etapa titulo="Editorial review"><p>According to the minimum review required for the series (see the next section).</p></Etapa>
  <Etapa titulo="Reports"><p>Faculty content report and external report, where the series requires them.</p></Etapa>
  <Etapa titulo="Submission to the Editorial Committee"><p>With all review records and the Annex H checklist.</p></Etapa>
  <Etapa titulo="Publication"><p>With version, date, CRediT authorship, sources, licence and disclaimer.</p></Etapa>
  <Etapa titulo="Dissemination"><p>By the Communication and Marketing team.</p></Etapa>
</Etapas>

The Editorial Committee decides each submission with a reasoned decision: approve; approve with minor changes; return for revision; or reject. The Collegiate Board cannot overturn an editorial decision or waive review.

## Minimum review by output

| Output | Lead authorship | Minimum review |
|---|---|---|
| Observatory panel edition | N3 (Panel Lead) | Numerical verification |
| Data Insight | N2 | Numerical and editorial |
| Business-Cycle Note | N3 | Numerical and editorial |
| Economic Assessment | Collegiate Board | Factual and numerical |
| Semiannual Economic Report | Chief Economist, with Programme Coordinators | Numerical, editorial and faculty |
| Technical Note | N4 | Numerical, editorial and faculty |
| Discussion Paper | N4, with faculty supervisor | Numerical, editorial, faculty and external |
| Project Report | Project team | Numerical, editorial and faculty |
| Policy Brief | Authors of the source output | Editorial and faculty report on fidelity to the source |
| CEE Blog | N2 | Editorial; numerical when there are own data |

Length and purpose of each output are in the [catalogue of outputs](../publicacoes/).

## Editorial Committee reference deadlines

| Output | Review deadline | Maximum rounds |
|---|---|---|
| Panel edition, Data Insight, Blog | 5 working days | 2 |
| Business-Cycle Note, Policy Brief | 10 working days | 2 |
| Technical Note, Project Report | 20 working days | 3 |
| Discussion Paper | 30 working days, plus the external report | 3 |

## Common standards

<Cards cols={2}>
  <Card titulo="Identification" icone="fingerprint"><p>Every output has a series, a sequential number within the year and a version, as in <code>CEE-NT 2027/03 v1.0</code>.</p></Card>
  <Card titulo="Data, code and verification" icone="database"><p>Every published number can be re-run from the source data. When replication code is not public, the publication says: “Code available from CEE on request”.</p></Card>
  <Card titulo="Charts and tables" icone="line-chart"><p>Number, informative full-sentence title, unit, note where needed and source with period; no 3-D charts, no unmarked truncated axes, and colour is never the only way to tell series apart. On this site every chart also has an alternative table and a CSV file.</p></Card>
  <Card titulo="References" icone="book-marked"><p>Author-date system (ABNT NBR 10520:2023 and NBR 6023:2018); outputs in English may follow APA.</p></Card>
  <Card titulo="Language" icone="scroll-text"><p>Clear language, short sentences, one idea per paragraph, technical terms defined on first use; no unsupported adjectives or partisan language. Facts, interpretations and scenarios are kept apart.</p></Card>
  <Card titulo="Recommendations" icone="scale"><p>Only in Technical Notes, Discussion Papers, Project Reports and the Policy Briefs derived from them, in a separate section. Other outputs describe and analyse; they do not recommend.</p></Card>
</Cards>

## Vintages

Official series are revised by their sources, so every extraction is archived with its date (a *vintage*), allowing published results to be reproduced with the data as they stood. On this site raw data are kept with their extraction date and a checksum and are never edited.

## Errata, expressions of concern and retractions

A material error in a published output triggers a **notice linked to it within seven calendar days** and an erratum with date, version and effects on results and conclusions as soon as the correction is done.

<Cards cols={3}>
  <Card titulo="Erratum" icone="rotate-ccw"><p>Material error: notice within seven calendar days and an erratum with date, version and effects on results and conclusions.</p></Card>
  <Card titulo="Expression of concern" icone="triangle-alert"><p>When a relevant doubt is under investigation. Authors have five working days to respond.</p></Card>
  <Card titulo="Retraction" icone="ban"><p>When reliability, authorship or regularity are substantially compromised. It records reasons, scope and history, without presuming personal fault.</p></Card>
</Cards>

See the [errata register](../erratas/).

## Authorship, conflicts and AI

Authorship follows the CRediT taxonomy; reviewers, verifiers and supervisors are acknowledged unless they meet the authorship criteria, and honorary authorship is forbidden. Conflicts of interest are declared on joining, every semester and whenever a new situation arises; a link to an organisation directly assessed bars the person from preparing, reviewing or deciding on the output. The use of AI tools is disclosed in each output, with the tool and purpose; responsibility stays with the authors, AI tools are never listed as authors, and personal or restricted data are not entered into external tools.
