## Editorial workflow

Every output follows, in this order, the workflow set in the Internal Rules (art. 46): (1) **registration of the proposal** with the Programme Coordinator, stating the question, sources, period and method, and the Manual question it answers; (2) **preparation** with data and code in a CEE repository, with version history; (3) **numerical verification** by someone other than the authors, who re-runs the code and checks every number, table and chart, with a verification report; (4) **editorial review**; (5) **faculty and external reports**, where required; (6) **submission to the Editorial Committee** with all review records and the Annex H checklist; (7) **publication** with version, date, CRediT authorship, sources, licence and disclaimer; (8) **dissemination** by the Communication and Marketing team.

The Editorial Committee decides each submission with a reasoned decision: approve; approve with minor changes; return for revision; or reject. The Collegiate Board cannot overturn an editorial decision or waive review.

## Common standards

- **Identification.** Every output has a series, a sequential number within the year and a version, as in `CEE-NT 2027/03 v1.0`.
- **Data, code and verification.** Every published number can be re-run from the source data. When replication code is not public, the publication says: "Code available from CEE on request".
- **Charts and tables.** Number, informative full-sentence title, unit, note where needed and source with period; no 3-D charts, no unmarked truncated axes, and colour is never the only way to tell series apart. On this site every chart also has an alternative table and a CSV file.
- **References.** Author-date system (ABNT NBR 10520:2023 and NBR 6023:2018); outputs in English may follow APA.
- **Recommendations.** Only in Technical Notes, Discussion Papers, Project Reports and the Policy Briefs derived from them, in a separate section. Other outputs describe and analyse; they do not recommend.
- **Disclaimer and licence.** "The views expressed are those of the authors and do not represent the position of the Pontifical Catholic University of Minas Gerais." The Economic Assessment adds that it expresses the Board's collegiate judgement. Licence CC BY 4.0, unless restricted by third parties.

## Vintages

Official series are revised by their sources, so every extraction is archived with its date (a *vintage*), allowing published results to be reproduced with the data as they stood. On this site raw data are kept with their extraction date and a checksum and are never edited.

## Errata, expressions of concern and retractions

A material error in a published output triggers a **notice linked to it within seven calendar days** and an erratum with date, version and effects on results and conclusions as soon as the correction is done. An expression of concern is issued when a relevant doubt is under investigation; a retraction when reliability, authorship or regularity are substantially compromised, after giving the authors five working days to respond. See the [errata register](../erratas/).

## Authorship, conflicts and AI

Authorship follows the CRediT taxonomy; reviewers, verifiers and supervisors are acknowledged unless they meet the authorship criteria, and honorary authorship is forbidden. Conflicts of interest are declared on joining, every semester and whenever a new situation arises; a link to an organisation directly assessed bars the person from preparing, reviewing or deciding on the output. The use of AI tools is disclosed in each output, with the tool and purpose; responsibility stays with the authors, AI tools are never listed as authors, and personal or restricted data are not entered into external tools.
